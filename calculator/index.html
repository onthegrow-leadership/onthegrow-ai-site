Work Redesign Value Calculator

Most AI tools count hours saved. This one shows the net value a year the redesign delivers.

What this is forBefore you spend on AI, know what redesigning the work is worth. This calculator takes one team, shows which tasks go away, which AI helps with, and which stay with people, and turns the freed hours into net value a year. It then discounts that number for the things that slow results down: adoption, readiness, leader review, and mistakes. You leave with a one-page case your CFO can check.

Who it is forCEOs, CHROs, CFOs, and operating leaders deciding where to start with AI.

Type your numbers in the white boxes.The shaded boxes calculate.

Step 1. Your team: what an hour costs

What one hour of this team costs you: salary plus benefits and payroll taxes, divided by hours worked. Example: $85,000 in salary plus $25,000 in benefits and taxes, over 2,000 hours, is $55 an hour.
