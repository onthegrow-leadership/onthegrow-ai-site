EBITDA added, year by year

EBITDA added

EBITDA added is net of the annual AI tool cost.

How the number is built

  1. Freed hours. Each task's weekly hours times the net freed rate for its placement.
  2. Conversion. Avoided hires and cut spend are valued at loaded cost. Revenue capacity is valued at revenue per hour times contribution margin. Unassigned time counts as zero.
  3. EBITDA added. The converted value, scaled by the ramp for each year, minus the annual AI tool cost.
  4. Value at exit. EBITDA added in the exit year times the exit multiple.

Most AI calculators count hours saved and stop. This one subtracts the time it takes to check the work and counts only the time leadership puts to use.
